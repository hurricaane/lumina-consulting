# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

E-commerce brand founders doing 20K-80K/month on Shopify. They've invested in product, branding, and community, but their site doesn't reflect that investment. They're looking for a technical partner who understands their business, not just a developer who writes code. They visit the site to evaluate whether Lumina is credible, competent, and worth a 30-minute discovery call. Context: browsing on laptop or phone, often comparing against other agencies or freelancers. French-speaking, based in France.

## Product Purpose

Marketing site for Lumina Consulting, a studio that builds custom e-commerce sites for growing brands. The site must accomplish three things: (1) make the visitor feel understood by naming their exact frustrations, (2) demonstrate technical credibility without using jargon, (3) convert qualified prospects into discovery calls. Success is a visitor who thinks "they get my problem" within 10 seconds and books a call within one session.

## Brand Personality

Refined, Trustworthy, Modern. Confident without being loud. Technical depth expressed through clarity, not complexity. Premium tone that respects the visitor's intelligence. French copy: direct, empathetic, zero jargon. Vouvoiement always.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS/startup aesthetic: gradient blobs, stock illustrations, "Schedule a demo" energy, Intercom/HubSpot template vibes, identical card grids with generic icons
- Overdesigned agency: Awwwards-bait with parallax everything, 3D blobs, scroll hijacking, style over substance, animation for animation's sake
- Cheap freelancer portfolio: template-looking, Wix/Squarespace energy, clip-art icons, "I build websites" hero, visible Bootstrap/template DNA
- Words to avoid in copy: artisan, pixel, chaleur, magie, passion, digital, bespoke, atelier, rêve, âme, écosystème

## Design Principles

1. **Credibility through restraint.** Every element earns its place. Whitespace, typography, and rhythm do the heavy lifting. If it looks effortful, it's overworked.
2. **Show the rigor, hide the complexity.** The site should feel technically precise (fast loads, smooth transitions, clean layout) without ever explaining _how_. The craft is the proof.
3. **Empathy before persuasion.** Name the visitor's problem before presenting the solution. The structure mirrors how trust actually builds: recognition, then credibility, then invitation.
4. **Motion with purpose.** Animations signal hierarchy and guide attention. Nothing bounces, nothing elastic, nothing that plays while the visitor is trying to read.
5. **One voice, no seams.** Every page, section, and micro-interaction feels like it came from the same hand. Consistency across the site is non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance. 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI components. Keyboard navigation support. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Semantic HTML structure. French language with proper `lang="fr"` attribute.
